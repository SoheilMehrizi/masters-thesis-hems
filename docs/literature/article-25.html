Eight points cover tool, argument, action, memory write/read, reflection, stop and model routing. Five modes are REPLACE, CASCADE, ASSIST, GUARD and TRIGGER. The cascade accepts the top option only above a decision-specific threshold; free-text arguments remain generative. The central strength is measuring isolated, encountered-state and live-task performance separately and releasing a typed interface, training recipe and evidence ledger.
